# Chart Editing

Users can create and edit charts to display and compare products.

1. To create a new chart, click the **+** icon. This will add a blank chart to the section.
    <img src={CreateChart} alt="create_chart" style={{width: 1000}} />
    <br /><br /><br />

2. To edit charts, expand the chart options then click **Edit**. This will open the editing viewer.
    <img src={ExpandOptions} alt="expand_options" style={{width: 400}} />
    <br /><br /><br />
    <img src={EditChart} alt="edit_chart" style={{width: 400}} />
    <br /><br /><br />

3. Add product layers by clicking the **Add Product** button.
    <img src={Products} alt="products" style={{width: 800}} />
    <br /><br /><br />

4. Add Y axes by clicking the **+** icon. Add product layers by clicking the layer icon then selecting the defined product.
    <img src={DefineYAxis} alt="define_y_axis" style={{width: 800}} />
    <br /><br /><br />


Define multiple product layers and add them to the defined Y axes to show multiple products on a single chart.    
    <img src={Example} alt="example" style={{width: 800}} />
    <br /><br /><br />

Charts can be saved by clicking the **Done** button at the top right corner of the editing viewer.
